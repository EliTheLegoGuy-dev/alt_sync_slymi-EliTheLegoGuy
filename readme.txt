The macro automatically saves when a setting is changed.

The boosting aid has delayed hotkeys like any normal one should have. It also has an "auto holder," which
is a replacement for an auto clicker. When you right click, it holds down the left mouse key, so that
your tool collects without risking any crazy auto clicking stuff (it's useful during challenges and 
boosts). The gumball warning turnes the sync window cyan when your gummyballer is at 800x and pink at
900x, so you don't have to actively watch the gumball stack.

The hive hub tab is useful for idling or macroing in the hive hub so you can touch grss. It can do a more
or less normal pattern on loop to collect (you will lose nectars), but it can also post trade requests
either every time the collection macro re-aligns itself or, if that's turned off, every minute. The accept
trades option does as it says and accepts trades to you whenever sombody requests one. It resets the macro
after the trade is accepted. The shiftlock option activates the shift lock when gathering. Make sure to
enter your player movespeed without buffs so the macro moves your character the correct amount (the macro
travel distance does not compensate for buffs).
